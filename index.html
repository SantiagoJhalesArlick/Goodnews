Romans Road & John's Way (KJV 1611)

Where Will You Spend Eternity After This Life?

Read the Romans Road, John's Way, Scripture warnings, grace by faith alone, and eternal assurance in Christ.

1. Romans Road: We Are All Sinners Romans 3:23 • James 2:10

Every person has sinned, and breaking even one point of God's law makes us guilty.

“For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God;”

— Romans 3:23 (KJV 1611)

“For whosoever shall keep the whole law, and yet offend in one point, he is guilty of all.”

— James 2:10 (KJV 1611)
2. The Wages of Sin & Judgment Romans 6:23a • Rev. 21:8

Sin earns spiritual death and the second death unless forgiven by God.

“For the wages of sin is death;”

— Romans 6:23a (KJV 1611)

“But the fearful, and unbelieving, and the abominable, and murderers, and whoremongers, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all liars, shall have their part in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone: which is the second death.”

— Revelation 21:8 (KJV 1611)
3. Christ Died & John's Way Rom. 5:8 • John 3:16 • 14:6

God proved His love by sending His Son Jesus Christ, the only way to the Father.

“But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.”

— Romans 5:8 (KJV 1611)

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.”

— John 3:16 (KJV 1611) • John 14:6
4. Saved by Grace, Not Works Ephesians 2:8–9

Salvation cannot be earned by human works or boasting—it is God's free gift by grace through faith.

“For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God:”

— Ephesians 2:8 (KJV 1611)

“Not of works, lest any man should boast.”

— Ephesians 2:9 (KJV 1611)
5. Trust & Call Upon the Lord Romans 10:9–10, 13

Believe in your heart that God raised Jesus from the dead, confess Him as Lord, and call upon His name.

“That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation.”

— Romans 10:9–10 (KJV 1611)

“For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.”

— Romans 10:13 (KJV 1611)
6. Assurance of Salvation 1 John 5:13 • John 10:28

Those who believe on the Son of God can know right now that they have eternal life.

“These things have I written unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know that ye have eternal life, and that ye may believe on the name of the Son of God.”

— 1 John 5:13 (KJV 1611)

“And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand.”

— John 10:28 (KJV 1611)

Prayer to Receive Christ & Eternal Life

Romans 10:9–10, 13 • 1 John 5:13

If you want to turn from sin and trust in the Lord Jesus Christ alone for salvation today, pray sincerely from your heart:

“Lord Jesus, I know I am a sinner and cannot save myself by my own works. I repent of my sin and believe that You died on the cross for my sins and rose again. I trust in You alone as my Lord and Savior today. Thank You for saving me and giving me eternal life. Amen.”
